## 🎯 WHY THIS ORDER?

1. **Hero** – Immediately establishes trust (critical for conversions)
2. **Services** – Answers "What do you do?" quickly
3. **Mid-CTA** – Second ask (some users ready to convert early)
4. **Why Choose Us** – Builds confidence for fence-sitters
5. **How It Works** – Reduces objections/friction
6. **Drive with Us** – Secondary conversion (driver recruitment)
7. **FAQ** – Answers remaining questions (perfect before deciding)
8. **Contact Banner** – Final push before leaving
9. **Footer** – Last chance with strong CTA + info

## 📱 MOBILE STICKY BUTTONS

**Behavior:**
- Hidden on page load (don't block hero)
- Appear after user scrolls 400px down
- Sticky to bottom of screen
- 2 buttons: Call (blue) + WhatsApp (green)
- Full width on mobile, hidden on desktop
- 20px bottom padding on main to prevent content overlap

**When to show:** Perfect for users who:
- Scrolled past hero but need quick action
- Looking at services/FAQ and ready to contact
- On mobile (where navigation is harder)
