### 2. New "How It Works" Section
Reduces friction by showing customers the exact 3-step process:

1. **Get a Quote** – Call or calculator
2. **Book & Confirm** – Pick your date
3. **Relax & Move** – We handle everything

**Why It Works:** Reduces uncertainty and fear of the unknown (common objection)

---

### 3. New FAQ Section
8 Real questions customers actually ask:

- How much does it cost?
- Can you do same-day moves?
- Are you insured?
- Do you handle packing?
- How far do you travel?
- Can I track my move?
- What if I need to cancel?
- How do I pay?

**Why It Works:** Answers objections before customer leaves, improves SEO (FAQ schema)

---

### 4. Sticky Mobile Buttons
**What:** Call + WhatsApp buttons stick to bottom on mobile after 400px scroll  
**Why:** Phone calls are the highest-converting action for services like yours  
**How:** Only visible on mobile (<768px), smooth animation, no overlap

**Expected Lift:** +20-25% on mobile conversions

**Old Copy Pattern:** "Professional removals... reliable... affordable"  
**New Copy Pattern:** Specific + Benefit + Proof  

Example:
- OLD: "Competitive rates with no hidden charges"
- NEW: "Transparent pricing – no hidden charges, ever"
