## J) GOOGLE ADS OPTIONS (All Budget Levels)

### **1. Google Local Service Ads (LSA) - BEST for Beginners**
- **Budget:** £50-£200/week (pay per lead, not click!)
- **Cost per lead:** £10-£25
- **Benefits:** 
  - Google Guarantee badge (instant trust)
  - Appear at TOP of search (above regular ads)
  - Only pay when someone calls/messages
  - No creative needed (just profile)
- **Setup Time:** 2-3 days (background check required, £30 one-time)
- **Setup:** https://ads.google.com/local-services-ads
- **Expected:** 3-15 leads/week depending on budget
- **Best for:** "man with a van near me", "removals falkirk", "furniture delivery"

**Why start here:** Pay for RESULTS, not impressions. Highest conversion rate (60-70%).

- **Setup Time:** 1-2 hours
